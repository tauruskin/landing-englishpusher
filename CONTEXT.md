# Domain Context — landing-englishpusher

## Glossary

- Interim copy — neutral placeholder text on the landing page before Tetiana's content exists; it makes no promises about price, "free", outcomes or results. NOT content Tetiana has approved (that arrives with the sales-content feature).
- Landing lead — a trial lesson request sent from the landing page and recognisable as such by the page address recorded with it. NOT a lead from the app hub (`app.englishpusher.in.ua`), even though both go into the same CRM form.
- Landing page — the public sales page for Tetiana's lessons at `landing.englishpusher.in.ua`. NOT the app hub (`app.englishpusher.in.ua`) and NOT the Moodle site (`englishpusher.in.ua`).
- Operator — Oleksandr, who builds and maintains the landing page, its tracking and the lead handling (CRM, Leads Bot, weekly report). NOT Tetiana, who decides what the page offers and promises.
- Prospective student — an adult stranger (an IT/office professional or a general adult learner) who reaches the landing page and has not yet sent a trial lesson request. NOT an existing student, who already takes lessons and uses Moodle or the app.
- Test form — a CRM form with the same fields as the live "Trial lesson request" form that no lead handling reads (no deal, no Leads Bot card, no report line; the CRM still records a contact, which the Operator deletes); everything except the published Landing page sends to it. NOT the live form, which only the published Landing page and the app send to.
- Tetiana — the teacher and business owner; she takes the trial lessons and decides positioning, prices and what the page promises. NOT the Operator.
- Trial lesson request — a filled-in trial form that the CRM has confirmed it received; it becomes a contact, a deal and a Leads Bot card. NOT merely opening the form, and NOT a submission the CRM has not confirmed.
